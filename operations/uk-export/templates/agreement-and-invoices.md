# Invoice setup checklist
Use real supplier/client identity, unique sequential invoice number, issue/due dates, project reference, exact service description, currency, tax breakdown only where applicable, total due and actual supported payment methods. Ask an accountant about overseas B2B tax treatment. Never promise tax exemption or VAT status without confirmation.
Deposit description: “50% deposit — [package], agreed scope [date/reference]”.
Balance description: “Final 50% — [project], approved [date], payable before custom-domain launch”.
Send Stripe-hosted invoice link from actual account, or separately verified business bank details. Never place bank details or secrets in this repository. Mark paid only after confirming cleared funds in the payment account, not a screenshot supplied by a customer.
