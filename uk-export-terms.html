UK export website scope

These fixed-price packages are for business customers. The completed written quote confirms deliverables, tax treatment, provider costs and dates before you pay. Read alongside the general Terms, Privacy and Refunds policies. Any variation must be agreed in writing before acceptance.

£295 — UK Trade Showcase

One responsive static page, up to 6 sections and 6 product families, one language, adaptation of supplied English copy, company credentials and one agreed enquiry route. Existing domain or client-funded new domain. No domain registration allowance.

£695 — Full UK Catalogue

Up to 5 static pages OR one expanded page up to 10 sections, up to 12 product families, 2 specification tables and 6 supplied PDFs. Includes standard available non-premium .co.uk registration for the first year up to £20, registered to the client. Domain availability and actual cost are checked before acceptance. No ecommerce, stock management or custom product database.

Included in both

Two consolidated revision rounds within scope, mobile layout, basic page titles/descriptions and sitemap, agreed hosting/domain configuration, and source-file handover after full payment. You approve factual content and the final preview in writing.
